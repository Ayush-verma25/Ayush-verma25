## 👋 About Me

I'm a **motivated, detail-oriented Full Stack Developer** from **Ajmer, Rajasthan, India 🇮🇳**, specialising in the **MERN stack** (MongoDB, Express.js, React, Node.js) and modern frameworks such as **Next.js** and **TypeScript**.

I build complete, production-style applications — from database design and REST APIs to responsive UIs — and I deploy them live. My projects go beyond tutorials: they include **role-based dashboards, online payments (Stripe & Razorpay), authentication, background job scheduling, and AI-powered features**.

- 🔭 Building full-stack products with real-world workflows (booking systems, marketplaces, SaaS, LMS)
- 🌱 Currently exploring **AI-integrated apps**, **Next.js 15**, and **system design fundamentals**
- 💞️ Looking to collaborate on projects that help me grow into a stronger engineer
- ⚡ **Open to work:** Full Stack Developer / MERN Stack Developer roles (internship, full-time, or freelance)
